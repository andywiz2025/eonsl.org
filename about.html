About EON-SL

Independent observation for credible elections

EON-SL works to promote transparent, inclusive, peaceful and credible electoral processes in Sierra Leone and beyond.

Who we are

A civic and democratic accountability network

Elections Observer Network Sierra Leone (EON-SL) is a civic organization committed to enhancing election quality, strengthening democratic institutions and promoting informed participation across the country.

Our work includes election observation, civic education, community engagement, stakeholder coordination, and the documentation of election-related developments.

We believe that credible elections require transparency, inclusion, impartiality, public confidence and active citizen engagement.

Vision & mission

Guiding our work

Our Vision

To be a trusted and credible civic platform that promotes transparent, inclusive and peaceful democratic governance in Sierra Leone and beyond.

Our Mission

To strengthen the credibility of elections and democratic processes through independent observation, civic education, public engagement and stakeholder collaboration.

Mandate

What our mandate includes

Observation

Monitoring electoral processes and documenting events and developments linked to democratic participation.

Education

Providing citizens with practical information on rights, responsibilities, institutions and civic participation.

Engagement

Working with communities, local partners and stakeholders to encourage peaceful and informed participation.

Accountability

Supporting democratic accountability through credible documentation, reporting and stakeholder engagement.

Core values

The principles we work by

Integrity

Upholding honesty, objectivity and professionalism in all our work.

Inclusion

Ensuring diverse participation and equitable access to democratic information and opportunities.

Non-partisanship

Maintaining independence and impartiality in observation and civic engagement.

Accountability

Promoting responsible governance and democratic oversight through evidence and public engagement.

Aims & objectives

Our goals for democratic participation

Promote democratic confidence

Strengthen confidence in the integrity and credibility of the electoral process.

Build civic awareness

Equip citizens with the knowledge needed to exercise their rights, vote responsibly and participate meaningfully.

Support peaceful transitions

Encourage peaceful engagement before, during and after elections.

Document and inform

Collect evidence, share findings and support informed public discourse around electoral issues.
