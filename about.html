About EON-SL

Who We Are

Elections Observer Network Sierra Leone is an independent, impartial and non-partisan consortium observing the processes and procedures of the electoral cycle in Sierra Leone.

Our Identity

An Independent Electoral Observation Network

EON brings together civil society, faith-based, professional, youth and women’s groups to support citizen participation and electoral observation.

Elections Observer Network (EON)

Elections Observer Network (EON) is an independent, impartial and non-partisan consortium of civil society groups, faith-based, professional bodies, Youths and Women’s groups to observe the processes and procedures of the electoral cycle in Sierra Leone.

EON was formed in June 2019 and launched in September 2020 with 180 members regardless of political affiliation.

June 2019Formation of EON
17 September 2020Formal launch of EON
180Members at launch, regardless of political affiliation
Our Journey

From Formation to Regional Reach

Key milestones supplied by EON describing its formation, launch, national coordination network and expansion beyond Sierra Leone.

JUNE 2019

EON Formed

Elections Observer Network was formed as a consortium focused on observing the electoral cycle in Sierra Leone.

17 SEPTEMBER 2020

Formal Launch

EON was formally launched with 180 members regardless of political affiliation.

NATIONAL NETWORK

Regional & District Coordination

The Network succeeded in installing Regional and District coordinators across the country to facilitate the easy deployment of observation teams for elections.

REGIONAL EXPANSION

Established Beyond Sierra Leone

EON currently states that it has been established in Liberia, Côte d’Ivoire and Guinea.

Our Mandate

Mobilising Citizens. Supporting Credible Elections.

Our mandate is to mobilize citizens to actively participate and to complement the efforts of the Election Management Bodies (EMBs) in ensuring a free, fair, inclusive, peaceful, transparent and credible elections.

Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Is a democratic society where transparent, inclusive and credible elections exist for the furtherance of the wishes and aspirations of the citizens

Our Mission

Our mission Is to work in partnership with all Election Management Bodies (EMBs) in enhancing citizen’s participation in the electoral processes

Our Work

Aims & Objectives

Key aims and objectives stated in EON’s organisational material.

Encourage citizen participation throughout the electoral process.
Critically analyse systematic electoral activities undertaken by ECSL, PPRC, NCRA & Statistics Office.
Promote free, fair, inclusive, transparent and accountable elections.
Prevent electoral fraud.
Promote issue-based campaigns.
Enhance the credibility of electoral outcomes.
Identify successes and challenges and make recommendations to Election Management Bodies.
