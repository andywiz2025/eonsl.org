Who We Are

Independent observation for credible elections.

EON-SL is an independent, impartial and non-partisan consortium of civil society groups, faith-based organisations, professional bodies, youth and women's groups observing processes and procedures throughout the electoral cycle in Sierra Leone.

EON-SL's mandate is to mobilize citizens and complement Election Management Body efforts toward free, fair, inclusive, peaceful, transparent and credible elections.

Our Focus

Thematic Areas

EON-SL's work is organised around key areas supporting democratic governance and electoral integrity.

01

Credible, Transparent & Democratic Good Governance

Supporting democratic governance, transparency and accountability.

02

Elections & Electoral Reforms

Observing electoral processes and contributing to discussions on electoral reform.

03

Voter Registration, Education & Participation

Promoting informed and meaningful citizen participation.

04

Peace & Human Right Education

Supporting peaceful participation and awareness of rights.

05

Women's Empowerment through Political Participation

Encouraging women's participation throughout electoral processes.

06

Research, Monitoring & Evaluation

Generating evidence and recommendations from observation and monitoring.
