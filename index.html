Who We Are

Independent observation. Citizen participation. Credible elections.

Elections Observer Network (EON) is an independent, impartial and non-partisan consortium of civil society groups, faith-based, professional, youth and women’s groups observing electoral processes and procedures in Sierra Leone.

EON works to mobilize citizens, complement the efforts of election management bodies and contribute to free, fair, inclusive, peaceful, transparent and credible elections.
