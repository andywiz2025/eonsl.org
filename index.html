Elections Observer Network Sierra Leone (EON-SL) supports transparent, inclusive, peaceful and credible electoral processes through independent observation, civic education, documentation and public engagement.

Our Focus

Supporting credible electoral processes

EON-SL works with citizens, communities, electoral stakeholders and civil society to strengthen informed and peaceful participation.

Election Observation

Independent observation and documentation of electoral processes in accordance with EON-SL's mandate.

Civic Education

Promoting informed citizen participation, electoral awareness and understanding of civic responsibilities.

Public Engagement

Working with communities and stakeholders to encourage peaceful, inclusive and constructive democratic participation.

Who We Are

Elections Observer Network Sierra Leone

EON-SL is an independent, impartial and non-partisan consortium of civil society groups, faith-based and professional bodies, youth and women's groups observing electoral processes in Sierra Leone.

The network mobilizes citizens to participate in electoral processes and complements electoral management institutions through observation, civic education, documentation and public engagement.

Our Approach

From observation to public reporting

A structured observation process supports consistent documentation, analysis and follow-up.

Observe

Monitor electoral activities and conditions in the field.

Document

Record relevant observations using established procedures.

Analyse

Review information and identify significant findings.

Report

Communicate verified findings through appropriate reports.

Recommend

Present practical observations and recommendations where appropriate.

Follow Up

Engage relevant stakeholders on documented findings and recommendations.
