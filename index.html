Who We Are

A citizen-focused network committed to credible elections

Established in 2020, EON-SL is a consortium of civil society groups, faith-based organisations, professional bodies, youth and women's groups committed to observing electoral processes in Sierra Leone.

EON works to mobilise citizens and complement the efforts of Election Management Bodies in advancing free, fair, inclusive, peaceful, transparent and credible elections.
